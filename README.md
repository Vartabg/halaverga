# Halaverga · Return to Earth

A browser flight study through Meridian, a fictional modern hillside city damaged in 2033 and visited in 2113. Assisted free flight, water skimming, cancellable landing, first/third person, touch and keyboard controls, and one clearly fictional municipal record.

The reconciled version combines the Blender district and storm atmosphere with the current shooting, controls, Envoy suit, open-world flight, pause interface and voting. Branch choices and verification are recorded in [the version reconciliation](docs/integration/2026-10-09-reconciled-versions.md).

## Run and verify

Use Node 22 and pnpm 10.32.1. Run `pnpm install`, then `pnpm verify`. Use the local managed-preview tool to start `pnpm exec next dev --hostname 127.0.0.1 --port 3366`. Production browser checks require `pnpm build` and a managed `next start` preview on port 3366, followed by `pnpm test:browser`. CI runs `pnpm verify` — TypeScript, unit/physics tests and the production build — on ubuntu; the browser and accessibility checks stay Mac-recorded because their frame-timing thresholds need a real GPU (see `docs/verification.md`).

The local Playwright configuration uses system Chrome on macOS, never Chrome for Testing. `pnpm test:a11y` fails on automated AA violations. Test results, traces and reports are excluded from Git.

## Controls

- Trackpad only (desktop default): click the open scene to lift and cruise; move the pointer to steer. Hold near an edge to keep turning; the turn keeps going for as long as you hold (since 2026-09-25). Passing over the header keeps steering, sliding off the side of a window keeps turning for up to 6 s, and leaving through the top or bottom flies straight after 1 s. Two-finger scroll up accelerates and down slows. Click again (or press Space or Escape) to brake and hover. While hovering, click and drag to look around; aim at a nearby flat surface and click Land. No held click, pointer capture mode, or keyboard is required. With the blaster on, one thing changes: while hovering or on the ground, a click fires at the centre reticle when you let go (dragging only looks), so Space starts flying instead, and W does too once you are in the air. Click again, or press Space, to stop. Hold C to fire and Q to aim at any time. Saves from before 2026-09-24 that held One finger + keyboard return to this default ([decision](docs/DECISIONS.md)).
- Optional trackpad profiles, chosen in the Controls sheet: One finger + keyboard (captures the pointer, slides to look, flies with WASD, click to fire; `/?trackpad=simple`, see [docs/simple-controls.md](docs/simple-controls.md)), Captured steering, and the Flow scroll experiment. `/?trackpad=free` returns to the default.
- Keyboard: WASD move; arrow keys look; R/F rise/descend; Space lift/land; Shift toggle Surge; Escape pause; E read a nearby terminal. For captured mouse look, choose “Mouse + keys” in the Controls sheet and click the scene. Trackpad mode remains available if mouse capture is unavailable.
- Touch, one thumb (the default again since 2026-09-26; details in [docs/touch-controls.md](docs/touch-controls.md)): hold anywhere in the open scene to lift and cruise, slide to steer (left/right turn, up/down climb and dive), drag farther to go faster, hold near an edge to keep turning, let go to hover. A second thumb makes left move / right look. Tap a drone to blast it; a tap on empty space does nothing. No Fire or Aim button, no auto-fire.
- Touch, two thumbs (opt-in: Twin stick in the Controls sheet): the left thumb puts down a floating joystick wherever it lands on the left side (push farther for speed, hold straight up to boost, double-tap to cruise); the right thumb drags anywhere to look. Big Fire, Aim, Rise and Descend buttons sit within thumb reach, clear of the screen edges. Rise lifts off and climbs; hold Descend over flat ground to land. Flight is level unless "Fly where I look" is on. Look speed, left-handed layout, control size and opacity are in Flight settings (Pause, then Flight settings).
- On touch screens, during play Safari's scroll, pinch-zoom and long-press gestures are blocked, and the game pauses only when you actually leave it (app switch, tab switch, page hide). A back swipe shows a "Leave the game?" card; progress is saved. Paused, the page and the Field guide pinch-zoom normally; the page has no zoom-limit metadata. Add to Home Screen opens it full screen. None of this is validated on an iPhone yet.
- Optional tap controls provide short directional movements and separate view buttons without dragging. They are hidden by default to keep the scene clear; turn them on in Flight settings (Pause, then Flight settings; under More controls while the blaster is on).
- Suit blaster (on by default; plan in [docs/plans/2026-09-22-shooter.md](docs/plans/2026-09-22-shooter.md)): rogue drones to shoot. On touch screens tap a drone (two thumbs: hold Fire, and the auto-fire assist also fires when the crosshair rests on a drone). On the default trackpad, a click while stopped fires (see above); One finger + keyboard and Mouse + keyboard click to fire once the pointer is captured. Shooting never slows or steers flight: there is no look friction and no speed cap (2026-09-26). On touch screens and in One finger + keyboard or Mouse + keyboard, short hints in the one hint line under the top row teach one step at a time and never repeat (a 6 s tip you missed shows again after Resume until you take off); the default trackpad states its controls in one line at the bottom of the screen. Aim (Q), the shot magnet and tap controls sit under More controls. Turn it off in Flight settings, or add `?shooter=0` (or `?shooter=1`) to the URL for one session. While it is on, the explorer's right hand is an arm cannon (original model, `scripts/arm_cannon/build.py`; records in [docs/art/arm-cannon](docs/art/arm-cannon/README.md)), carried low and snapped onto the crosshair when you fire; with it off the explorer is exactly as on main.

## Controls demo and vote

- Ten ways to fly are available in one place. While you play, the top of the screen is one row: the altitude readout on the left, then (when it works) **Vote**, **Controls** and **Pause**. **Controls** opens the one sheet with your device's list, and it is the only place that list lives: five on touch (One finger, Twin stick, Draw, Conduct, Brush) and eight on desktop (Cursor, One finger + keys, Flow, Captured, Mouse + keys, Draw, Conduct, Brush). Switching is instant, never pauses, keeps your position and is saved. The pause card and Flight settings each have a Controls row that opens the same sheet. The Field guide and Flight settings are rows on the pause card (press Pause), not buttons in the top row. On a desktop keys 1-8 pick the controls in that order; the checkbox "Number keys 1-8 switch controls" turns them off. `?controls=<id>` applies one for a visit. The old four-style bar and keys 1-4 are gone. How each control works, and how "Tried X of N" is counted: [docs/controls-demo.md](docs/controls-demo.md). The Draw, Conduct and Brush details: [docs/gesture-lab.md](docs/gesture-lab.md).
- Getting away from a limit is easier (2026-09-28, node math and desktop browser checks, not felt on a phone): near a wall, the sky, the water or a hill a pinned suit turns its own heading toward open air (your own edge hold, arrows or cursor edge always win), the stop is one soft 1.1 s ease from surge instead of half a second, the district faces show as grids, and the screen names the fix (EDGE AHEAD · HOLD SCREEN SIDE TO TURN, then just EDGE AHEAD). A third finger that lifts no longer leaves the hand dead. Reviewed and fixed 2026-09-29. See [DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md).
- Turning all the way around is easier in every style (2026-09-25): fast phone swipes turn further and a thumb resting near an edge keeps turning, the desktop edge hold keeps turning, Conduct and Draw wrap all the way around, and Brush has a whirl stroke (a big loop) that spins you around. The turn times in the docs are node math on the real modules, not device measurements.
- The vote asks one question, "Which way of flying felt best?" (round r3). Fly at least two ways for 20 seconds each (until then there is no Vote button: two small dots on **Controls** fill in as ways are flown), then tap **Vote** (it appears beside **Controls** only when the vote works, and again in the Controls sheet and on the pause card), tap the one that felt best or **Can't tell**, and tap **Send vote**. Touch and desktop are counted separately. It is anonymous and unverified (no sign-in, no cookies, no text box), and it is the one thing that sends what you picked, only when you press Send. The game page also sends anonymous visit and step counts to Vercel Web Analytics, nothing at all when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. A vote over a limit is refused with a "busy" message; nothing is stored for it. Results are at `/results` (a ranking shows once enough people from enough networks have voted) and how a vote is counted is at `/privacy`. Backend (Upstash or Neon), privacy, limits, kill switch and launch steps: [docs/voting.md](docs/voting.md) and [docs/vote-runbook.md](docs/vote-runbook.md). Not checked on an iPhone, a real trackpad, or a real Upstash or Neon store yet.

## Architecture and original assets

Next.js/React hosts a lazy-loaded R3F/Three.js WebGL2 scene. Rapier's kinematic capsule sweeps handle collisions. `game/Player.tsx` owns motion; `game/CameraRig.tsx` owns the camera; keyboard, trackpad and touch share `runtime.ts` input. Preferences and safe checkpoints are stored locally. No account, database, or model request is needed to fly; only the optional vote card talks to a server.

The suit and camera share one interpolated physics anchor. Ten skeletal joints blend through acceleration, streamlined flight, turns and braking. The body points along its travel direction with yaw and pitch bounded near the view, so the chase camera keeps its back in view; banking is a roll about the travel axis. See [hero-facing-review.md](docs/hero-facing-review.md). Exterior building volumes, advance clearance sweeps and contact-corrected velocity prevent entry into unfinished interiors. The district perimeter brakes approaching flight; supported landings and saved checkpoints are checked against the actual geometry. The Field guide includes a route map. `docs/navigation-audit.json` records the finite route and high-speed regression evidence; it is not an exhaustive guarantee of every trajectory.

The district uses an authored Blender GLB with batched materials, procedural ground details and matching collision envelopes; its reachable horizon structures have triangle colliders. Sources and regeneration are in [the district art record](docs/art/blender-district/README.md). The suit is an original Blender model based on generated character references: regenerate with `Blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/build-suit.py`. The editable source is [art/halaverga-explorer.blend](art/halaverga-explorer.blend), including separate armor pieces, a weighted rig and packed references. [Character concept and exact prompt](docs/art/explorer-reference-prompt.md); [facial reference and exact prompt](docs/art/explorer-face-prompt.md). The GLB embeds two compact generated-reference texture atlases. No third-party character models, recordings, or game assets are included. Dependencies retain their respective licenses.

## Playtest status

The explorer uses a fitted graphite pressure layer, silver armor, restrained copper seams, green power insets and an exposed face. Its approximately 7.66-head-tall anatomy has balanced upper/lower limbs, modest hands and boots, and a distinct back. The 21-bone weighted GLB is 751,448 bytes, with 19,514 triangles and eight material batches. Skin and hair use embedded 256×256 and 128×128 PNG atlases; they add decoding but no separate HTTP requests. The player collider and camera remain independent of the model.

With a managed production preview running, `PLAYTEST_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3366 node scripts/review-flight-poses.mjs` renders front/profile/back and six chase views (hover, power flight, bank, climb, dive, brake) using the game's chase boom and view direction at a narrower field of view, with the actual playable rig in system Chrome, and checks both texture atlases decode. Set `SUIT_REVIEW_OUTPUT` to choose the screenshot destination. This temporary review page is intercepted locally, not a public game route. `Blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/render-explorer.py` renders the exported GLB in Blender. [Actual model study](docs/images/reference-hero-blender.png); [verification and limitations](docs/reference-hero-review.md).

This is a playtest candidate, not a measured iPhone release. Physical iPhone Safari, VoiceOver and subjective enjoyment checks must be recorded by the tester. Desktop viewport emulation and headless Mac timing are separately identified. See `docs/verification.md` for evidence and remaining checks.

Run `node scripts/profile.mjs` against a production preview for a five-minute desktop route sample. `PROFILE_OUTPUT` selects the report folder; `PROFILE_SECONDS` sets duration; `PROFILE_SURGE=1` uses maximum-speed flight. A first frame after resume is excluded; reports retain the most recent 18,000 active frames. These reports stay on the device until downloaded.

## Deployment

The dedicated Vercel project is `halaverga-flight` in `garo-vartabedians-projects`. Link explicitly before deploying from a new worktree. Use its generated test domain and retain immutable deployment URLs for comparisons. The game itself needs no environment variables. Voting needs one store from the Vercel Marketplace: Upstash for Redis (`KV_REST_API_URL` and `KV_REST_API_TOKEN`; `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL` and `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN` also work) or Neon Postgres (`DATABASE_URL`, used only when no complete Upstash pair is set), and `VOTE_SALT` is **required** (at least 32 random characters, never the store token). Without a store or a valid salt the game works and voting reports that it is not set up; preview deployments never vote unless `VOTE_ALLOW_PREVIEW=1`. Setup steps, including the required Firewall rate-limit rule, are in [docs/vote-runbook.md](docs/vote-runbook.md); the design and the limits are in [docs/voting.md](docs/voting.md). Never commit values or `.env` files. Hand-deploy steps, the post-deploy checks and the rollback target are in [docs/deploy.md](docs/deploy.md); the launch order and the owner-only clicks are in [docs/launch-readiness.md](docs/launch-readiness.md). The purchased Halaverga domain is a later launch decision.
