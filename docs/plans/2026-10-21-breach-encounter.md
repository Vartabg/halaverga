# Aerial breach encounter

Garo asked to build the researched encounter: useful high and low routes, flight that permits stopping and aiming, and a warden fight with weight. Preserve the approved angel/abyss setting and quick lesser-demon chains. This is an authored encounter pass, not a claim of finished production art or physical iPhone performance.

## Space and decisions

- The central60m-wide nave remains body-clear through the fight. The stone is both visible geometry and actual collision.
- The lower cloister runs from49m left of center at15m altitude, fromz12 to-106. Its inner wall and roof block a committed volley. Both ends and its outer side are open; it has enough room to stop and look. Pale blue entrance lights distinguish it from the gold upper route.
- The upper cantilever at43m right,70m flight altitude has a solid landing structure, underside ribs and clear dive approaches. Keep the established marked landing at30,61.44,-38. Height provides an approach angle; it does not grant damage or automatic counter credit.
- The caster alternates higher and lower repositioning goals between exchanges. The existing swept navigation remains the sole enemy translation owner. Tells and earned recovery remain stationary.

## Control and combat

- Standard no-input flight settles into hover; scene drags look without thrust. Hold Fly explicitly requests travel and drag steering; release, cancel, rotation and pause clear it. Existing held dives can gain speed and pull up; explicit Brake remains available.
- The first landed hit can provoke a warden and break its first layer. The final layer requires an opening earned by defense. Repeated normal contacts repel without depleting that layer. Two defended counter contacts can still finish from full armor.
- Preserve committed rush/cleave versus volley/splitfire responses, deliberate relocation, swept contacts and collision cover. Ordinary strikes, counters and banishments use the same player and camera owners.
- Actual elite contact gets a110ms player contact beat, versus45ms for lesser demons. Reduced motion bypasses the freeze. A failed guarded contact gets a shorter70ms beat. Recovery posture and the hit pose communicate resistance without moving the physics body through scenery.
- Elite banishment leaves a quiet sealed patch and gold marker below and progressively changes the breach's color. Victory still requires all eight banishments. Reset/unmount clears the visual aftermath; reduced motion uses a static reveal.

## Readability

Route guidance lives inside the existing objective panel. The opening instruction expires after five active encounter seconds and yields to warden instructions. Duplicate guard text yields to the warden card. Large foreground route hoops were rejected during inspection and replaced with small edge lights.

## Validation

Use real held controls and touch events in both phone orientations; do not teleport or inject combat success for browser acceptance. Check route traversal, projectile blocking, high approach visibility, hover while looking, two-move/two-counter warden exchanges, chains, interruption, graphics recovery and automated accessibility. Run the production budget and secret gates. Preserve source-linked fiction distinctions and one camera/body writer. A physical iPhone playtest and uncoached enjoyment study remain necessary.
