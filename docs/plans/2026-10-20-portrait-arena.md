# Portrait arena and enemy rhythm

Garo's feedback: the improved character exposes a cramped, obstructed stage; portrait should support substantial play, and fast banishments have little anticipation or payoff. His explicit pacing choice in this chat is **quick lesser demons, longer elite fights**. The following dimensions and timings are this implementation's first playtest proposal, not an assertion that he approved final tuning.

## Space

Treat the stage as a broad aerial court, oriented from the launch dais toward the breach. The center is for combat; large architecture gives bearings around the perimeter. The low causeways sit outside the center, with three perimeter sanctuaries instead of five dense rows of pillars. Keep the spawn and landing coordinates. Replace the tall altar pedestal with a thin floating platform so the escape routes below it remain open. Keep background stone fragments low and outside the fighting area.

Sample a 90 m wide center (-45 to45), z40 to-125, y25 to54 using a 2 m Rapier sphere against the same triangles the player sees. Check main low/mid patrol sightlines from the approach and central chase positions. This is a sampled collision/sightline contract, not a claim that every camera pose is obstruction-free. All eight patrols now occupy a shared23–47 m fighting band; the high floating landing is optional traversal space. The former tower patrol no longer pulls chains to75 m and the ceiling. Portrait and landscape gameplay captures are required to judge the actual composition.

## Rhythm

Six lesser demons keep the existing one-contact finisher and fast chaining. Two wardens (patrols5 and7) carry two readable armor bands. Each armor contact breaks a band, pushes the angel a short distance back, and gives the warden a visible recovery/retaliation using the existing windup and fiery dart. The player can evade, raise the shield, rebuke to interrupt, or chain into another demon. Recovery is per enemy; it does not stop movement or impose a global attack cooldown. Repeated/queued commands cannot consume another armor layer during the response.

After the armor breaks, a final sword contact binds the warden for0.65 s, then casts it for2.4 s and seals the opening. Lesser timings stay0.18/1.1 s. The fall reaches beneath the abyss surface even from high altitude; a visible iris remains at the original cast position and closes after the body is gone. The seal uses a shrinking gold ring rather than only an expanding pop. Existing capped/muted audio voices layer armor contact, impact and closure. This remains game fiction, not a claimed literal reconstruction of scripture.

On the final elite contact, the angel settles into a hover and the single camera planner frames the cast origin through binding and the fall. Movement, manual look, Brake or a new strike releases that framing; the camera does not chase the falling body below the stage. Lesser kills retain carry-through.

The warden HUD appears only around a relevant warden and reports armor, response, exposed, bound and cast states. Introductory combat instructions disappear after the first action to reduce portrait clutter. No second camera writer, slow-time clock, wing changes or extra combat buttons are added.

## Later passes

Judge space and exchange rhythm first. Bespoke elite silhouettes, authored attack animations, more varied arena encounters, musical sound design, environmental narrative and physical iPhone comfort/performance still need their own measured passes. This is not finished AAA art or a claim that enemy variety is complete.
