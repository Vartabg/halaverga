# Hermon and the warning at the pass

Revision 1 · November 19, 2026. Source baseline 8c9b268 (the playable crossing) and 2427aac (regional relief on the same base). The working branch is codex/hermon-oath-pass.

Garo welcomed the freedom taken with the interception. He rejected the mountain's appearance and wants a playable geography, a plot woven into play, and a cleanup of conflicts left by older refactors. Hermon at dawn in the days of Jared, accessible assisted flight and a stable pilot-controlled heading are established. The warning and the particular landforms below are proposed game fiction. Whether the player wants a second chapter remains open.

## The flight

Make the route legible to someone coming to Hermon for the first time. Ignoring the landscape used to yield an 8/8 victory in the original encounter. A realistic sightseeing flight would invite the pilot to follow the ridge. A time trial through gates would reward shaving seconds. This chapter asks the pilot to read the mountain to help two people get a warning out.

Integrate the existing measured SRTM skyline and natural dawn on the task branch. Build one continuous landscape to the horizon from the same relief, sunlight, materials and ground surface the main game uses. Preserve the real regional orientation. In the foreground exaggerate two local karst forms. On the left, a sheltered snow hollow gives away the Watcher's first low pass. On the right, exposed limestone ribs and a wind-scoured shoulder show the later high line. Replace the crossing's expanse of dark water with a dry mountain floor and settled snow. Both routes must be visible before commitment. Give the southeast shoulder a small localized lift which influences the pilot's actual trajectory, witnessed by moving spindrift. A neutral pilot still misses. Keep collision, visibility and the central crossing in agreement; no new automatic camera steering. Reference the Mount Hermon Nature Reserve, the Hebrew University cave survey and the elevation provenance. This is a compressed playable pass inside measured regional relief.

## The warning

1 Enoch 6:4–6 places the Watchers' oath and descent on Hermon in Jared's days. The pilot, witnesses, pass and valley camp are invented. At dawn two witnesses carry word of the oath toward the families below. A Watcher breaks away after them. The terrain and the bird's movement communicate the mid-flight turn. Intercept it and the travelers can send their warning across the pass; a signal from the camp answers on screen. Let it reach the arch and the witnesses turn back while the camp stays dark. Use the outcome camera and travelers' action to show this while the player can immediately retry. Show the source beside the fiction on the opening and in an accessible text account. Use the existing flight state for outcome and elapsed time for staging; pause, reduced motion, retry and both endings must agree with text and scenery.

Give the story a direct, clearly named entrance on the normal home page. Preserve the established free-flight controls, guide, saves and alternative text reading. Avoid fetching story models or terrain for the landing page. Check both desktop and phone layouts.
