# Third-person shooter, phase 1

2026-09-22. This plan adds aiming and shooting that feel like popular third-person shooters. The tuning comes from measured maths, not guesswork.

## Owner decisions

- **Targets.** Rogue security drones left over from 2033. They hover, dodge, flinch when hit and break apart. They do not fire back in this phase.
- **Weapon.** An energy blaster in the suit's right forearm.
- **Always a cannon** (Garo, 2026-09-23). While the blaster is on, the right hand is replaced by an arm cannon at all times, built to the character's definition and proportion standards. See "Arm cannon and shot choreography" below.
- **Default.** The shooter is on by default. "Suit blaster" in Flight settings turns it off. `?shooter=0` or `?shooter=1` overrides the setting for one session.

Simple by default (owner-approved 2026-09-23; details in [simple-controls.md](../simple-controls.md)):

- Two verbs at the start, move and shoot, with the same model on every device: one hand moves, the other aims and shoots. Touch auto-fires; trackpad and mouse click to fire.
- **Hick's law** (fewer visible choices, faster decisions): a phone shows one play button by default (Lift/Land; Fire only with Auto-fire off), and one hint at a time.
- **Fitts's law** (big targets in the thumb zone): with Fire hidden, Lift/Land keeps main's large bottom-right spot.
- **Progressive disclosure** (lower cognitive load): Aim, hold/toggle aim, Aim assist and tap controls sit under Flight settings › More controls (tap controls only with the blaster on; with it off they stay where main has them); vent timing still works with a Fire button or C but is not taught (on touch it needs Auto-fire off); each hint appears only after the previous action is done and never repeats.

Other rules:

- Aim can be set to toggle instead of hold (More controls).
- No control uses a multi-finger trackpad gesture.
- Fire does not force aim, so the player keeps flying.
- Held controls clear on pause, blur, resize, rotation, lock loss, touch cancel and reset. On desktop only, they also clear on a trackpad pinch-zoom. iPhone Safari fires `gesturestart` for any second finger, so touch never uses that event.

## Movement while shooting

A player can track a target only while its angular rate v/d stays under about 30°/s. That gives three speed tiers:

1. **No shooting.** Flight works as today: surge up to 34 m/s.
2. **Hip fire** (firing, or within 0.3 s of a shot).
   - The flight target is computed as today, so thumb and trackpad cruise keep their chosen speed. Only the target's magnitude is clamped to 13 m/s.
   - Above 13.5 m/s the suit brakes at rate 9, at most 60 m/s², and reaches 13.5 m/s within 0.6 s.
3. **ADS in flight.** Hover-strafe at 6·min(1, 2|input|) m/s, so half a stick throw is full speed.
   - Vertical speed is (R/F + 0.5·sin(pitch)·forward)·4 m/s. Touch players can therefore climb by aiming up.
   - Approach rate 12, capped at 110 m/s²: 34 m/s falls to 6.5 m/s within 0.5 s.
   - ADS walking is ×0.7.

Releasing ADS resumes normal flight at the existing 42 m/s² ramp.

## Feel constants

**Weapon**
- Camera-ray hitscan at 9 shots/s.
- A press fires on the frame it is seen, from a frame-loop clock (dt ≤ 0.05 s).
- Heat is 4.5 per shot, so the weapon overheats on shot 23 (2.44 s). Cooling starts 0.3 s after the last shot, at 60/s.
- Overheat locks the weapon for 1.6 s. Pressing Fire in the 0.22 s window centred at 0.8 s vents heat to zero.

**Spread**
- Cone diameter = `lerp(0.8°, 4°, spreadHeat/10) × lerp(1, 0.4, ads) × moveMul`.
- moveMul is 0.7 hovering, 1 up to 13 m/s and 1.8 at 34 m/s (rate 5).
- The first shot is exact in ADS while hovering, after 0.25 s of rest.
- Spread heat rises 1 per shot and recovers at 12/s after 0.15 s.
- Samples use radius u^1.5·half, from a seeded generator.

**Camera**
- ADS blend `a` moves at rate 20 in and 14 out, and snaps to exactly 0 or 1 within 1e-4. Every blend and spring does the same, so an idle shooter leaves look, camera and pose bit-identical to main.
- FOV = baseFov − 15a + punch. baseFov is damped separately (rate 3): 65° at the hip, 50° in ADS.
- ADS look gain is tan25°/tan32.5° = 0.732.
- ADS boom: t = clamp((aspect − 0.46)/0.54, 0, 1); z = lerp(3.4, 2.6, t); y = lerp(0.55, 0.45, t); x = min(0.85, 0.6·z·tan25°·aspect). This keeps the right shoulder at least 20% of the half-width from centre.
- View settle rate is 40 in combat and 15 otherwise.

**Recoil**
- Exact spring with ω = 10, ζ = 0.6 and impulse 20·A. A is 0.35° on mouse and 0.2° otherwise; yaw is ±0.4A.
- It peaks at 0.998A at 116 ms, and sustained fire climbs to 1.8A.
- Per-shot punch also comes from channels that leave aim alone:
  - the arm cannon and whole-body shot choreography (below);
  - a 1.12× crosshair pop (1.06× from the 4th shot of a burst);
  - the Fire button pressing in to 0.92.
- Removed 2026-09-23: the per-shot FOV micro-punch (it moved the frame edge only about 2 px and made a fifth visible channel) and the old forearm recoil spring, which the shot choreography replaces.

**Kills**
- 80 ms hit-stop on the drone only.
- trauma += 0.45·max(0, 1 − d/20); shake = trauma² × 2.5°. The shake is rotation only, uses seeded 15 Hz noise and decays at 1/s.
- No FOV punch (removed 2026-09-23: at most 6 kill channels); the gated #fff2c0 pop stays.

**Aim assist**
- Active only while aiming, firing, or within 1 s of a shot.
- No rotational pull, because it would steer the flight heading.
- Friction zones are the drone's angular radius plus 0.6°/2.0° at the hip or 0.4°/1.2° in ADS, scaled with FOV.
- Base friction strength is 0.6/0.5 at the hip and 0.7/0.4 in ADS. It ramps in at 60/s and out at 4/s.
- The device profile and the strength setting are applied when each look() delta arrives, so switching device takes effect at once.
- There is no friction when the input follows the target's drift.
- Magnetism adds 1.5° at the hip or 0.75° in ADS to the angular radius. It is full strength to 60 m and zero at 100 m, multiplied by (1 − 0.5·bloom), and needs line of sight.

| Profile (follows the steering device) | Friction | Magnetism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Touch | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Tap controls | 0 | 1.5 |
| Trackpad | 0.6 | 0.75 |
| Mouse | 0 | 0.5 |

The setting offers Off, Standard (1) and Strong (1.5). Total slow is capped at 0.85.

## Drones

**Patrols**
- Five drones: terrace-greeter (the tutorial drone), terrace-wing, canal-mid, viaduct-gap and tower-orbit.
- tower-orbit circles 8.7 m above the tallest roof.
- They fly Lissajous paths at 3–5 m/s, at least 5.7 m from city colliders and 5 m from the route. They have no Rapier colliders.

**Damage**
- 6 HP: a body hit does 1, an eye hit does 2.
- The eye has radius 0.32 m and sits 0.72 m forward. It counts as the hit whenever the ray enters it before leaving the body, and only from the front.
- Time to kill: 0.56 s on the body, 0.22 s on the eye.

**Alert**
- Triggers within 70 m with line of sight: one ray per drone every 0.2 s, at most 3 alert at once.
- The drone anchors where it noticed the player, orbits that anchor at 3 m, and the eye tracks the player.

**Telegraph and dodge**
- Trigger: the aim ray dwells within the angular radius + 2.5° (hip) or 1.5° (ADS) while the player is engaged. The dwell is 0.35 s on mouse or trackpad, 0.55 s on touch and 0.8 s on tap controls. Each near miss adds 0.15 s.
- Telegraph, 0.22 s: a white ring expands from the eye. That shape appears in no other phase. At the same time a chevron on the HUD crosshair points toward the drone, a rising chirp plays, and the drone pre-tilts. A quadrotor must tilt by atan(a/g) before it can accelerate, so the cue is physically honest.
- Dodge: 4 m perpendicular to the aim ray over 0.35 s on a smootherstep curve (peak 21 m/s). The path is checked for clearance first.
- Punish window: 0.7 s after the dodge. The drone holds at its dodge position until its orbit centre returns to the anchor.
- Cooldown: 3.5 ± 0.5 s (4.5 s on touch).
- Only one drone dodges at a time. An eye hit during the telegraph cancels the dodge. The tutorial drone does not dodge until the first kill.

**Flinch and breakup**
- A hit knocks the drone 0.25 m along the shot and wobbles it ±8°.
- The white hit flash passes the same 3-per-second gate as the muzzle. After three flashes the drone holds a steady tint instead.
- At 3 HP a shell plate breaks off, sparks and smoke trail, and the eye pulses slowly (1.5 Hz).
- A kill splits the drone into 3 large and 6 small pieces (g = 22), with a 250 ms fireball and 1.2 s of smoke. Pieces that reach the water ring it.

**Readability**
- Each eye halo keeps at least 10 CSS px on screen out to 85 m.
- An amber rim lights the gunmetal body against the ruins.

**Respawn**
- 8 s after a kill, the drone flies in from about 50 m along a clear path, with a panned chirp.

## Look and sound

**Palette**
- Player energy: #f2feff core with a #58e1ff fringe, additive and not tone-mapped, so ACES keeps the hue.
- Drones: gunmetal #3a4048, amber eye #ffb347, red-orange #ff5a36 when alert.
- Sparks white to #ffd08a; water #9fe3d6; hit smoke and drone trails #4a4450.
- Kill burst (approved deviation, 2026-09-23 review): a 50 ms #fff2c0 pop (its own 3-per-second gate; none under reduced motion), a flame core that holds its brightness about 150 ms while it grows 1.2 → 3.5 m and cools #ffd27a → #ff7a2a → #8a1c0c, 20–28 sparks with a 2 px floor, debris that glows orange for 0.6 s, and a pale #8c8794 plume (6 puffs, 1.2 → 4 m over 1.8 s, rising). The old 0.25 s brown disc and #4a4450 smoke did not read as an explosion.
- Glow comes only from procedural halo sprites. There is no bloom and there are no new lights.

**Tracers.** A retracting beam that arrives within 33 ms and lives until arrival + 70 ms. The tail eases in from the muzzle over the whole life, so every shot starts at the arm; the last 70 ms fade as (1 − k)². Width is max(0.05 m, 1.5 px). Pool of 8. A miss draws a ≥ 6 px glint at its end point for 80 ms (not under reduced motion). Approved deviation (2026-09-23 review): the old fixed 18 m slug was sub-pixel beyond about 40 m.

**Muzzle flash** (approved deviation, 2026-09-23 review: the 0.2 m, 3-per-second flash barely showed)
- Every shot: a 50 ms flash sized in screen pixels, a 36 px core (≤ 0.45 m) and an 80 px #58e1ff halo at 0.45 alpha (≤ 0.9 m). It stays under 90 × 90 px at any camera distance, below the WCAG 2.3.1 small-area limit, so it needs no gate.
- Anything larger, the 0.9 m bloom, keeps the 3-flashes-in-any-1-s gate.
- The steady emitter glow is 40 px (≤ 0.3 m) at 0.6–0.9 alpha, warming from cyan to orange with heat. Overheat steam is 6 puffs, 0.2 → 0.8 m at 0.45 alpha over 1.1 s.

**Audio**
- Procedural Web Audio on its own context: at most 8 voices, behind a limiter.
- The shot layers a noise transient, a 1100→150 Hz saw, a sine punch and a tail.
- Hits tick at 2 kHz, brighter on the eye. Kills are 3 dB louder and rise one semitone per chain kill, up to +5. Telegraph and arrival chirps are panned.
- Audio follows the existing mute setting. It unlocks on a click, key press or touch release, and only while the blaster is on and sound is on: with the blaster off (or muted) no context is created and the audio session is never touched, as on main.
- When sound is on, the audio session is set to "playback" where Safari supports it, so the silent switch does not mute the blaster. Pause, mute, leaving and a hidden tab suspend the context and set the session back to "auto"; the next gesture resumes it. Turning the blaster off (or a fault) closes it.
- The landing page holds only the unlock (src/ui/audioUnlock.ts). The voice graph (src/ui/audioBus.ts) loads with the scene.
- The first shot while muted shows "Blaster sound is off · Settings" once.

## HUD

- **Crosshair.** Four ticks at the true spread radius, tan(half)/tan(fov/2) × H/2. The ticks turn amber and tighten by 20% when a centred shot would connect: inside the shot's own magnet cone (device profile, ADS, bloom, 60–100 m falloff) or on the drone's body. Approved deviation (2026-09-23 review): the outer friction zone lit amber where mouse and trackpad shots almost never hit. Friction still uses the outer zone.
- **Heat.** A 270° arc at 22 pt, going from cyan to amber to red. During lockout a marker sweeps the arc and the vent window lights up.
- **Hit markers**, shown on the same frame as the hit:
  - animation: scale 1.4 to 1.0 over 70 ms, hold 90 ms, fade 140 ms;
  - white X for a hit, amber notched X for a weakpoint, grey X when blocked: 2.5 × 8 px ticks 11–19 px from the centre (outside the drone's silhouette, inside the heat ring) with a 1 px dark edge;
  - kill (approved deviation, 2026-09-23 review): a larger #ff3b30 diagonal X, 1.15×, never rotated (a 45° turn put it on the crosshair's axes as a red plus), fading until 450 ms.
- **Controls hint.** Progressive, one instruction at a time in the band under the header, clear of the crosshair, the suit and the thumbs; each step retires once its action is done and the progress is saved, so it never repeats. Tap controls and the free, captured and Flow trackpad profiles keep a single 6 s line. Steps, copy and rules: [simple-controls.md](../simple-controls.md).
- **Flight messages** move to the same band, below the controls hint, while the blaster is on, so no message covers the muzzle.
- **Telegraph chevron.** Points toward the drone that is about to dodge, for 0.35 s.
- **Score.** A ×N chain for 1.2 s, with no persistent score.
  - "Drones downed" appears on the pause card.
  - A throttled "Drone down" live region sits outside the aria-hidden HUD.

## Accessibility and reduced motion

**Reduced motion removes:** the ADS zoom and boom change, recoil kick, shake, every per-shot body spring, the core flare, the raise overshoot, the kill beat, the vent flick, the idle sway, the press-frame swing snap, tracers, the muzzle strobe and the eye pulse.

**It also removes** the crosshair's per-shot pop, the kill pop and the miss glint, and keeps the kill plume to two still puffs.

**It keeps:** ADS accuracy, look gain and hover, hit markers (without the pop), small sparks, the kill core and audio.

**Flash limits apply to everyone,** not only under reduced motion: the muzzle and drone flash gates, and the slow eye pulse.

**Controls:**
- Buttons are labelled and at least 44 px. Hit boxes include the extension and stay 24 px or more from the safe-area edges.
- The HUD is pointer-events:none.
- Fire works from the keyboard or a switch. Aim toggles on its own pointerup (a second-finger tap makes no click) and from the keyboard.
- Tap controls cover players who cannot drag. With them on, the pad's buttons are named "Fire toggle, stops after 3 seconds" and "Aim toggle", the separate touch Aim is hidden, and the pad's empty cells pass touches through. They sit under More controls with the blaster on and in main's place with it off; with the blaster on, tap players never see the drag hint.
- More controls is a native `<details>` disclosure in Flight settings (keyboard and screen-reader operable, 44 px summary). It exists only with the blaster on. It holds tap controls, the touch Aim button, hold/toggle aim and Aim assist, and opens by itself for anyone already using one of them. On touch screens the Suit blaster section (Auto-fire) comes first in Flight settings. It is checked with axe (WCAG 2 A/AA) open.
- Fire's edge turning starts only once the finger has left Fire's hit box, so a thumb resting on its rim never turns the view.
- Arrow keys (blaster engaged): a fresh press turns at 30% for 150 ms, then full speed, both times the ADS gain. With the blaster off, arrows run main's exact code.

## Robustness

- Every shooter frame callback is wrapped in a guard, because React Three Fiber does not catch exceptions in useFrame.
- An exception turns the shooter off, resets its feel state and shows "The blaster stopped. Flight continues." The camera and the render keep running.
- A React error boundary covers render errors.
- Turning the setting off, or unmounting the shooter, resets aim assist, blend, springs and the muzzle to exact rest.

## Performance budget

- With the shooter off, the game is bit-identical to today.
- Budget: at most 15 extra draw calls (drones 5, shot effects 2, impacts 5, arm cannon 3) and at most 14k extra triangles (pools about 7.6k, arm cannon 2,808 of a 4k cap). Main is at 575,774 of 600k, so the scene stays near 587.4k. Justification for the +3 / +4k: the cannon is three meshes on one material (the slide and the vent hatch move), with no shadow draws (a shadow texel is about 0.107 m, wider than the cannon, and the suit already shadows the forearm). Raised from 12 / 10k on 2026-09-23 with the owner's always-a-cannon decision. The drones' halo and telegraph-ring pools draw only the instances they show, so an empty pool issues no draw call.
- No new lights, no shadows on the pools, no per-frame allocation, seeded randomness only.
- Rapier rays:
  - one aim ray per frame, only while aiming or holding the hip-fire pose, plus the HUD's blocked-muzzle ray on alternate frames, so aiming costs 1.5 rays a frame on average (approved deviation, arm fix);
  - one shot ray plus one muzzle ray per shot;
  - 25 line-of-sight rays per second.
- Shooter UI, audio graph and settings stay out of the landing page's first load: ShooterHud, FireControls and ShooterSettings are lazy chunks (the HUD and Fire are warmed once the setting is on; with it off nothing is requested). Accepted exception: runtime.shooter's plain state and its input functions (combat.ts, no three/Rapier) stay on the landing page, because runtime.ts and the landing input handlers own it. `scripts/check-first-load.mjs` fails on minification-safe blaster markers and above a 636 KB budget (main 614.9 KB). The PR #12 merge plus one-finger firing reached 636.0 KB (2026-09-23); Flight settings (TestPanel, TrackpadSettings) then became a chunk warmed after Begin, bringing the landing to 628.7 KB.
- `PROFILE_SHOOTER=1 node scripts/profile.mjs` records the budgets by hand (desktop high tier only).
- Measured 2026-09-23 with the arm cannon (production build, headless system Chrome on an M2 Max Mac, 1440×1000, high tier, 45 s route each; not iPhone): shooter off peak 15 draw calls / 575,774 triangles; shooter on peak 25 / 584,774, so +10 draw calls and +9,000 triangles, inside 15 / 14k. Frame time p50 16.7 ms and p95 16.9 (off) / 16.8 ms (on), no stalls over 50 ms.

## Arm cannon and shot choreography (2026-09-23)

Garo asked for the right hand to be an actual weapon, built to the character's standards, with a shooting feel tuned to what mobile-shooter players measurably enjoy and the whole body moving in time with every part of the shot. He chose **always a cannon**: while the blaster is on, the right hand is replaced by an arm cannon (buster style), the clearest silhouette on a phone. The rules that still hold: blaster off is bit-identical to main; original assets built by scripts in Blender 4.0.2; modules under 200 lines, no per-frame allocation, seeded randomness, reduced motion honoured; at most 3 flashes per second for everyone. Every number below comes from public/models/suit.glb or was measured on 2026-09-23 (lead design, revision 2, which answered 13 review objections).

### Forearm contract and the asset

- The cannon is modelled in the bind frame of forearm_r (identity rotation, origin at the forearm_r head, three.js axes, metres) and parented rigidly to that bone. `src/world/cannonContract.ts` holds the shared numbers: AXIS_ORIGIN, BARREL_AXIS (12.3° off the bone line), MUZZLE at s = 0.46, HAND_HEAD, the stations, rimStation(dz), the chase/ADS VIEWS, the palette and the Suit/ArmCannon handshake (`cannonDrive`, `cannonLink`).
- Faces: **top** = bind −Z (world-up while aiming and carrying), **outer** = bind +X, **underside** = bind +Z. The chase camera sees the cannon from behind, 12–17° toward the top/outer faces, so everything meant to read at chase (core lens, heat strips, copper fins) sits on the top/outer quadrant.
- `scripts/arm_cannon/build.py` builds `public/models/arm-cannon.glb` reproducibly (two builds, same sha256): 0.36 m long (1.38 heads), cuff about 0.125 × 0.14 m, barrel 0.085 m, stepped steel muzzle ring 0.094 m with a dark bore; core pod with a recessed lens (mask R) and two heat strips (mask G), and on the outer flank a heat-sink stack (two copper fins, mask G, framed by graphite vanes) that widens the rear outline to 0.169 m (visual review r3), vent hatch (hinged on its muzzle-side edge, opening to 105°) over a glowing cavity and five fins, slide shroud with two glowing coils over two rails, emissive muzzle lip and barrel collar (mask B). 2,808 triangles (cap 4k), 106,000 bytes, three meshes on one material with 8×8 palette PNGs and nearest filtering, no skin, no animation. Records: docs/art/arm-cannon/.
- Emissive: a shader patch sums mask.r·uCore + mask.g·uHeat + mask.b·uRing. Relaxed core #58e1ff × 0.25; aim raised up to 0.6; firing 1.0 plus a per-shot flare of +1.5 (τ 50 ms, toward #f2feff). The heat ramp uses the HUD thresholds (cyan, amber #ffb347 from 0.6, red #ff3a10 from 0.85, ±0.05 blends) with a 1.5 Hz ±25% swell (a ramp, never a blink). The core lens follows the heat ramp too (it is the largest glow seen from behind). Overheat: core red at 0.3, strips, cavity floor and fins fading red but holding 3/4 while the hatch is open, steam jetting sideways out of the hatch, one amber swell at 0.69–0.89 s, slide racked back. The lens covers 3–15 px, far under the WCAG general-flash area; reduced motion removes the flare.

### Runtime

- `ArmCannon` mounts inside ShooterLayer's error Boundary in its own Suspense, so the model is fetched only while the blaster is on and a load error turns the blaster off while flight continues. It parents a clone to forearm_r (joints[7]); `ready` is set only once it is parented; it is visible only while `ready && handHidden`. Shaders warm with `compileAsync(root, camera, scene)`. The runtime module is `cannonRuntime.ts` (an `armCannon.ts` next to `ArmCannon.tsx` clashed on case-insensitive disks).
- Suit's blaster block lives in `src/world/suitBlaster.ts`, inside one module-scope `guarded('Suit blaster', …)`. Hiding the hand: hand_r scaled to 1e-3 and the blaster skin swapped in, then `handHidden`. Off (or after a fault, unguarded): the original skin attribute objects, hand scale exactly 1, the body at rest. The old fist morph (`fistMorph.ts`) is deleted.
- **Two muzzles.** The gameplay muzzle (`runtime.shooter.muzzle`) is published by Suit from the solved pose, right after the aim solve and before any kick, so resolveShot's occlusion test and the HUD's blocked glyph never see recoil. The effects muzzle (`cannonLink.fxMuzzle`) is published by ArmCannon at −19 from the kicked cannon_muzzle node; ShotFx draws the flash, glow and each same-frame tracer tail from it.

### Carry, raise and lower

- **Carry pose** (blaster on, third person): clavicle_r (0, 0.05, 0), upperarm_r (0.7 + clamp(view pitch, ±0.4), 0, 0.4), forearm_r 0.9, hand_r 0 (r3: pitch 0.7 so the raise is a sideways punch-out and the recoil snap reads on its own; within 11.2° of the crosshair line on screen). Measured barrel error to the crosshair from carry, body facing the view: 3.5–3.7° for view pitch −0.4 to 0.3 (limit 4.5°), 5.9° at 0.5.
- **Raise.** fireHold is 1 on the press frame and the shot fires that frame, with no wind-up. The arm weight eases at 35/s toward max(ADS blend, fireHold) × ventAimScale: 0.44 on frame N at 60 Hz, 95% by 86 ms. The shoulder swing weight is max(weight, fireHold × ventAimScale), and the swing is barrel-exact: one minimum arc about the shoulder puts the barrel point at the target's distance onto the target (a turn about the shoulder keeps distances, so this is exact; plain muzzle-direction iteration converged at only about 0.5 per pass up close). Measured: barrel within 1.5e-6° of the crosshair line over 6,392 reachable cases, settled and on the press frame from carry; the upper-arm limits bind in 1.4% of the grid, where the body turn closes the rest. FlightPresentation (−30) reads this frame's fire input and pending press, so the torso squares up on the press frame.
- **Overshoot and lower.** A second-order follower (f 6, ζ 0.6, r 2) adds a 2–3° overshoot after arrival. The arm holds 0.8 s after the last shot, then fireHold decays at 9/s; the core dims on the same curve. Reduced motion: the swing equals the weight (no snap), same rates.

### Choreography: one shot clock

The clock is weapon.shots plus weapon.sinceShot, read at −20 right after the shooter step (−25). Suit's order each frame: clips, flight mix and living layer; advanceShotBody and the cannon drive; the pre-solve layer (breathing, the grounded brace, the torso kick and the hover reaction: spine, chest, pelvis and legs, which the solve keeps while its barrel-exact swing holds the cannon on the line, so the body's later motion never adds a second, late rise to the cannon); the aim solve; the gameplay muzzle; the post-solve layer (the arm snap, clavicle breathing, the vent arm/clavicle/neck/head, the head's roll cancel and nod, the kill beat, the off arm and the sway); the visual model offset; the hand hide; updateMatrixWorld. ArmCannon then publishes fxMuzzle and the vent mouth at −19.

### Armpit flap: decision

A skin-weight correction used only while the blaster is on, applied at runtime to the cloned suit geometry (`src/world/blasterSkin.ts` + `blasterWeights.ts`); suit.glb is untouched and blaster-off restores the original attribute objects. The cannon cannot cover the flap (the longest stretched edge is 0.15 m, under the shoulder, about 0.4 m from the cuff); its cause is arm/trunk weight sharing from rig.py's arm_share ramp, and fixing it in rig.py would change the blaster-off character in every raised-arm clip. Rules: R1 collapses the 1,033 anatomy hand vertices into the housing (hand_r) and the 153 undersuit cuff vertices to forearm_r; R2 drops leg weight from right-arm vertices; R3 relaxes the arm share of 892 armpit-band vertices harmonically (SOR ω 1.8, 49 sweeps). Measured stretched edges before → after: AIM_BASE 153/0.150 m → 6/0.055 m; aim grid worst 25/0.070 m (limit 25/0.08); flight extreme 319/0.188 → 39/0.077. Built once when the blaster turns on (about 30–40 ms alone). Measured on the wired pipeline: 0 of 1,186 original hand vertices outside the shell in 19 carry, aim, flight and vent poses; 84% of core-lens samples visible to the chase camera with the posed suit as occluder.
