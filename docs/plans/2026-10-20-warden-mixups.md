# Warden repertoires

User request: more than one attack per warden, varied choices as fights continue, updated phone QR.

Rift lancer: committed straight unblockable rush; close-range fixed-facing cleave that can be guarded, timed-parried, or escaped. Cinder cantor: three committed aimed darts; six split rays with a clearly marked safe central corridor and a visible recoil to give close players room. Both are original game fiction.

First turn teaches the signature. Next eligible turn teaches the alternate. Later choices use a reproducible weighted selector informed by distance and the previous defense, with at most two repeats when both moves are viable. Long-range lancer uses rush until the cleave is in range. After armor damage or two turns, tells shorten by150ms but remain at least1.1s; later volleys tighten their shot spacing by40ms. Aim still locks early, with no post-lock homing.

Each earned, physically landed counter removes one of two armor layers; the second binds and casts. The first reels the warden and provokes a different answer. Ordinary armor strikes/rebuke remain a fallback. Lesser enemies retain their quick chain loop. No new player/camera clock or writer, no simultaneous elite attacks, no extra rendering passes.
