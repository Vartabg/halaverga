# Sword combat that keeps moving

Based on `codex/momentum-flight`, retaining the CC0 angel and compact wings.

Click/C, or a tap/swipe on a demon, launches a physical sword strike. The ordinary demon is bound at contact, held for 180 ms, then cast and sealed automatically. Rebuke remains an optional interrupt (1); shield remains V or a held second finger. No circle is required. This compressed sequence is invented gameplay, not a scriptural combat procedure; the Field Guide distinguishes its imagery from the source texts.

One buffered press can select another demon during the dive. The buffer lasts 750 ms and launches after the impact beat, consuming exactly one request. It expires on a miss, cancellation, brake or reset. Finishing hits retain the existing 45 ms impact beat and carry velocity instead of parking at the victim. Contact uses the existing swept collision controller. The first launch selects a visible demon ahead. During a live chain, another press can select a nearby reachable demon around the angel, including behind it. Both reject dead, casting, distant and occluded demons, including enemies visible from the camera but blocked from the angel. It never teleports through stone.

A visible assisted target gets a gold screen marker. A phone Strike/Chain button keeps the next attack available when momentum has carried an enemy outside the screen. Reversing launches follow a spherical arc, preserving speed even across a 180-degree turn. Each contact advances a four-second chain count. The existing sword arc now stays at the contact point, with a pitched crack/thump and metallic edge. Binding/casting effects cannot replace the sword's contact cut with a ritual animation. A launch gently steers the requested view; manual thumb/held-pointer/arrow steering takes priority and reduced motion disables automatic view turning. CameraRig remains the sole camera writer.

Verification: the complete Vitest suite passes (204 files / 2,456 tests), including buffer expiry, cancellation, occlusion, reversing arcs, and preserving the sword cut during automatic binding/casting. Production build, TypeScript and the landing budget pass (9 scripts, 629.0 KB against a 629 KB limit). A real browser encounter reaches at least three consecutive contacts and three banishments using only keyboard strike presses or phone taps plus the Chain button. The counter stays visible in landscape as well as portrait.

The desktop controls test now clicks empty sky for its no-takeoff assertion: a click aimed at a reachable demon deliberately launches an attack, as required by this combat revision.
