# Combat camera handoff

Built on `codex/chain-combat`, preserving the angel, compact wings, momentum flight and automatic binding/banishment.

`CameraRig` remains the only writer of camera position and rotation. A pure combat planner tracks the live strike target, retains its current view when a chain changes target, and briefly holds the impact heading rather than spinning down after a banished demon. The framing boom eases out farther in portrait. During combat the boom follows the damped orientation as an orbit; it does not interpolate a straight chord through the angel on a reversing attack. The existing boom collision probe remains active. A second world sightline check shortens the boom if a pillar obscures the demon from the shoulder view even while the angel has a clear attack path.

Manual yaw/pitch input, including fine look movements, yields automatic targeting for 650 ms. The rig remembers its own heading handoff so that it cannot mistake that handoff for player input. Brake, reset, landing and attack cancellation release focus. Pause freezes it. Reduced motion disables the added framing/orbit, and first person retains the previous launch assist without the new boom motion. Outside combat, the planner returns the existing view and distance exactly. Transient state stays outside React.
