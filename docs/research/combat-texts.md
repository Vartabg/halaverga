# How the texts show demons being fought — research for combat design

Garo, 2026-10-19: "there needs to be research into how to combat these demons, which should be accurate based on textual evidence." This note collects what the texts actually say, keeps it apart from game fiction, and proposes mechanics that follow it.

Quotations are verbatim from the World English Bible (public domain, ebible.org/engwebp, fetched 2026-10-19) and R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch* (1917, public domain, Wikisource). 1 Enoch is a separate literary source, not part of the Protestant canon, and is labelled as such everywhere it is used.

## 1. What the texts say

### Demons are driven out by an authorised word, not by force
- Matthew 8:16 — "He cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were sick,"
- Mark 1:25–27 — "Jesus rebuked him, saying, “Be quiet, and come out of him!” The unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him. … For with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him!”"
- Luke 4:35–36 — "“Be silent and come out of him!” … “What is this word? For with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out!”"
- Acts 16:18 — "“I command you in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her!” It came out that very hour."
- Luke 10:17 — "“Lord, even the demons are subject to us in your name!”"

### The authority is borrowed — even an archangel does not rebuke on his own account
- Jude 9 — "But Michael, the archangel, when contending with the devil … dared not bring against him an abusive condemnation, but said, “May the Lord rebuke you!”"
- Zechariah 3:2 — "The LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, Satan!"
- Acts 19:13–16 — exorcists who borrowed the formula without the relationship: "The evil spirit answered, “Jesus I know, and Paul I know, but who are you?” The man in whom the evil spirit was leaped on them, overpowered them, and prevailed against them,"

### Demons react: they cry out and convulse before leaving
- Mark 1:26 (above); Mark 9:26 — "After crying out and convulsing him greatly, it came out of him."
- Mark 5:9 — "“My name is Legion, for we are many.”" — many spirits in one host.

### Some resist longer and need prayer
- Mark 9:29 — "“This kind can come out by nothing but by prayer and fasting.”" (Many early manuscripts lack "and fasting"; Matthew 17:21 is absent from many early manuscripts. Treat "prayer" as the secure reading.)
- Daniel 10:12–13 — "your words were heard. I have come for your words' sake. But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me twenty-one days; but, behold, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me"
- Daniel 10:20 — "Now I will return to fight with the prince of Persia."

### In the war in heaven the outcome is being thrown down, not killed
- Revelation 12:7–9 — "There was war in the sky. Michael and his angels made war on the dragon. The dragon and his angels made war. They didn't prevail. No place was found for them any more in heaven. The great dragon was thrown down … and his angels were thrown down with him."
- Luke 10:18 — "“I saw Satan having fallen like lightning from heaven."
- Revelation 12:11 — the victory belongs to the Lamb: "They overcame him because of the Lamb's blood, and because of the word of their testimony."

### Then they are bound, cast into the abyss and sealed
- Revelation 20:1–3 — "I saw an angel coming down out of heaven, having the key of the abyss and a great chain in his hand. He seized the dragon … and bound him for a thousand years, and cast him into the abyss, and shut it and sealed it over him,"
- Matthew 12:29 — "how can one enter into the house of the strong man and plunder his goods, unless he first bind the strong man?"
- Luke 8:31 — "They begged him that he would not command them to go into the abyss."
- Jude 6 — "Angels who didn't keep their first domain … he has kept in everlasting bonds under darkness"; 2 Peter 2:4 — "cast them down to Tartarus, and committed them to pits of darkness"
- 1 Enoch 10:4–5 (Charles) — "‘Bind Azâzêl hand and foot, and cast him into the darkness … And place upon him rough and jagged rocks, and cover him with darkness’"
- 1 Enoch 10:11–12 (Charles) — "‘Go, bind Semjâzâ and his associates … bind them fast for seventy generations in the valleys of the earth’"
- 1 Enoch 54:5–6 (Charles) — iron chains "prepared for the hosts of Azâzêl, so that they may take them and cast them into the abyss of complete condemnation … And Michael, and Gabriel, and Raphael, and Phanuel shall take hold of them on that great day, and cast them"

### Angels carry swords
- Genesis 3:24 — "cherubim … and a flaming sword which turned every way, to guard the way to the tree of life."
- Numbers 22:31 — "the LORD's angel standing in the way, with his sword drawn in his hand"
- 1 Chronicles 21:16, 27 — "a drawn sword in his hand" … "he put his sword back into its sheath."
- Ephesians 6:17 — "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God"; Revelation 19:15 — "Out of his mouth proceeds a sharp, double-edged sword"

### Defence: standing, and a shield that quenches fiery darts
- Ephesians 6:11–17 — "Put on the whole armor of God, that you may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. … taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the evil one." (Written to believers, not to angels — using it for an angel is adaptation.)
- James 4:7 — "Resist the devil, and he will flee from you."

### Angels fight as a host
- 2 Kings 6:17 — "the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire around Elisha."
- Matthew 26:53 — "more than twelve legions of angels"; Daniel 10:13 — Michael comes to help.

## 2. What the texts do not support (current build)
- **Holy fire shot from a bracer.** No text gives angels a ranged energy weapon. Angels hold swords; demons leave at a word.
- **Destroying demons.** Spirits are cast out, thrown down, bound and confined; Revelation 20 and 1 Enoch 10 keep them for a later judgement. "Banished" is right; explosions and death are not.
- **The angel's own power.** Michael says "May the Lord rebuke you!" Power is God's, not the hero's.

## 3. Proposed combat loop (game fiction built on the above)
1. **Rebuke** — a short spoken command (a word, not a projectile). The demon cries out and convulses (stagger). Grounded in Mark 1:25–26, Jude 9.
2. **Sword** — a flaming blade swept in arcs, "turned every way" (Genesis 3:24). Close-range, drives the staggered demon down.
3. **Bind** — a chain thrown around a demon that is down (Revelation 20:2, 1 Enoch 10:4, 54:3).
4. **Cast down** — fling the bound demon into the abyss, which seals over it (Revelation 12:9, 20:3, Luke 8:31). That is the "banishment".
5. **Shield of faith** — raised to quench fiery darts instead of only dodging (Ephesians 6:16).
6. **Princes** — a territorial prince withstands one angel (Daniel 10:13). The player must hold out until Michael comes to help. Boss design.
7. **"This kind"** — some spirits do not leave at a quick word; they need a held prayer (Mark 9:29).

Each mechanic should keep its source on screen or in the Field Guide, with game fiction labelled as such, as the tablets already do.
